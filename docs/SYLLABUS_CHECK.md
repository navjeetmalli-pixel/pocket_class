### Summary

Our CBSE Class 6-8 and Class 9-12 lists are in good shape. Chapter counts match the current NCERT books for Classes 6, 7, 8 and 9. Classes 10, 11 and 12 match the 2026-27 CBSE syllabus almost exactly. The 2026-27 syllabus keeps Class 10-12 on the existing NCERT books. Class 9 uses the new books (Ganita Manjari, Exploration, Understanding Society, Kaveri). Class 6-8 uses the Ganita Prakash, Curiosity, Exploring Society and Poorvi books. The real gaps are in three areas:

- **Classes 1-5.** Our chapter lists are topic-based and do not follow the current NCERT books. These are Joyful Mathematics, Mridang, Maths Mela, Santoor and Our Wondrous World (the EVS replacement).
- **Class 9 Social Science, Part 2.** The official syllabus and the book titles reported on the web disagree, and ours follows the web titles. This needs a check against the printed book.
- **A handful of small omissions in Classes 10-12.** For example, one Class 10 English story, a Class 11 English lesson that has been dropped, practical-work books in Geography, and some formative-only topics.

PSEB and the Hindi and Punjabi subjects could not be checked.

**Class 9 Mathematics.** The syllabus lists these topics: Number System, Introduction to Polynomials, Sequences and Progressions, Algebraic Identities, Linear Equations in Two Variables, Coordinate Geometry, Introduction to Euclid's Geometry, Lines and Angles, Triangles (congruence), Quadrilaterals, Circles, Area and Perimeter, Surface Area and Volume, Statistics, and Probability. The syllabus puts "Propositions and their converses" and algorithms inside other topics.
- Our titles (for example "The World of Algorithms" and "I'm Up and Down, and Round and Round") are book-style names and do not map obviously to Euclid's Geometry, Lines and Angles, Triangles or Circles.
- Check these four topics against the Ganita Manjari table of contents.

**Class 9 Social Science, Part 2.**
- The CBSE syllabus names the six themes: Oceans and Life; Life on Earth; Resistance and Resilience (1000-1700 CE); **India and the World-I (1900 BCE-1200 CE)**; **Authority**; From Ideas to Startups.
- Ours has "Governance and Public Policy" and "Managing Your Personal Finances" in places 4 and 5. A secondary source (Shaalaa) lists those two as the book's chapter titles.
- So the syllabus and the book titles disagree. We currently follow the book titles. Someone needs to open the printed book and decide, because the syllabus topics (Indian trade links; Danda and Nyaya) are different content from our chapters.

**Class 10 Science.** Ours has 13 chapters, which match the NCERT book. The 2026-27 syllabus also lists these topics, assessed formatively only:
- Periodic Classification of Elements. We have no chapter for it.
- Evolution. We have no chapter for it.
- Motor, electromagnetic induction and generator. These are probably inside our Magnetic Effects chapter.

The syllabus says Periodic Classification, Heredity and Evolution, and the Electric Effects topics are not assessed in the year-end exam.

**Class 10 English.**
- Missing: "The Book that Saved the Earth", the ninth story of Footprints Without Feet (ours stops at "Bholi").
- The syllabus also prescribes Words and Expressions II (Units 1-4 and 7-11). We have no chapters that follow this workbook.
- The syllabus calls the flying piece "Stories About Flying"; ours says "Two Stories about Flying". This is a trivial naming difference.

**Class 10 Social Science notes.** The syllabus says:
- Age of Industrialisation is assessed in periodic assessment only.
- Lifelines of National Economy is assessed by map-pointing only.
- Consumer Rights is project work.
- The Making of a Global World is partly board-examined and partly interdisciplinary project work.

None of this changes our chapter list, but it affects how we should weight them.

## What to do next, in order of impact

1. **Class 9 Social Science, Part 2.** The syllabus and the book disagree on themes 4 and 5. Open the printed book, decide, and rewrite the two chapters if needed. This is the only place where we might be teaching content that is not in the syllabus.
2. **Classes 1-5 Maths, EVS and English.** The current NCERT books are different from our topic lists. For store claims such as "follows NCERT", either re-map our chapters to the actual book chapters (Joyful Mathematics, Maths Mela, Our Wondrous World, Santoor, Mridang) or describe the app as topic-based and not tied to a book. The biggest mismatches are Class 1 Maths (11 vs 13 chapters), Class 3 Maths (13 vs 14) and Class 3 EVS (10 vs 12).
3. **Class 9 Mathematics.** Check that Euclid's Geometry, Lines and Angles, Triangle congruence and Circles each have a home in our 14 chapters.
4. **Class 10 English.** Add "The Book that Saved the Earth". Decide whether to cover the Words and Expressions II workbook.
5. **Class 11 English.** Remove "The Ailing Planet: the Green Movement's Role", or mark it as outside the syllabus.
6. **Class 10 Science.** Add short chapters for Periodic Classification of Elements and Evolution, marked "formative only", if we want to follow the syllabus fully.
7. **Class 12 Accountancy.** Check that "Redemption of Debentures" is not beyond the syllabus. Consider adding Computerised Accounting.
8. **Geography, Classes 11-12.** Add the practical-work books (Part I with 6 chapters, Part II) if we want full coverage.
9. **Minor formative-only topics.** Class 11 Maths (Principle of Mathematical Induction), Class 11 Biology (Digestion and Absorption), Class 12 Biology (Environmental Issues), Class 11 History (timelines and world history introduction).
10. **PSEB, Hindi and Punjabi.** Someone with a normal browser needs to check these. Open the PSEB syllabus page and the CBSE Hindi and Punjabi PDFs. Confirm whether Class 9 Hindi follows Ganga.

Everything not mentioned above matched at the level this report can check (chapter names and counts).
