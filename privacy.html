Privacy policy

Vidya study app · Last updated 29 October 2026

The short version

Vidya does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your name (if you type one), class, board, quiz scores, streaks, bookmarks and settings are saved only on your own phone or browser. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing the browser's site data deletes them.

Backups

If you choose Save backup, Vidya creates a file on your device and opens your phone's share sheet. Where that file goes (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp) is your choice and is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

Internet use

The phone apps contain all their content and work without the internet. The website downloads study files from this site the first time you open a class, then keeps them for offline use. No personal data is sent with these requests.

Children

Vidya is made for school students. Because it collects no personal information, it is suitable for children of all ages.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/navjeetmalli-pixel/vidya.
